We may collect your name, email address, shipping details, order history, and communication preferences when you use our site.

3. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your preferences, support website analytics, and manage consent for optional features.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with trusted service providers that help us fulfill orders or maintain our site.

Your rights
